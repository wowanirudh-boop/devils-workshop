## Profile
Runs the clock repair shop her father founded. Precise, stubborn, distrusts anything she
can't take apart. Talks to mechanisms more kindly than to people.

## Appearance
Ink-stained fingers; dark hair tied back with a length of clock wire.

## Voice
Short questions. Answers a question with a question when she's hiding something.
