## Premise
Brindle Cove is a fishing town on a salt marsh. Its harbour clock has kept time for the tides
for a hundred and forty years. One morning it stops at 3:12, and the town's clockmaker,
Mara Venn, finds it was stopped on purpose.

## Setting
Present day, late autumn. One harbour, one inn, one archive above the harbour office.
Everyone knows everyone's boat.

## Themes
Keeping time and keeping secrets; what a small town chooses to remember.
