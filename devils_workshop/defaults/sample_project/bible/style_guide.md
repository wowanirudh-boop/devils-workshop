## Voice
Close third person through Mara. She notices mechanisms, sounds and weather before people's faces.
Dry humour, never slapstick.

## Prose
- Short and medium sentences; one long sentence per scene at most, for a moment of wonder.
- Concrete nouns over adjectives. Name the tool, the fish, the type of rope.
- Dialogue carries the plot; tags are almost always "said".

## Avoid
- Characters explaining things they both already know.
- Ending a chapter on a summary of feelings. End on an action or a line of dialogue.
