## Summary
A page of the harbour ledger was recopied decades ago, and a name on it changed. Someone
in town has just worked this out, and stopping the clock is part of how they mean to prove it.

## ch2
Ada mentions, in passing, that one ledger has a page in different ink.

## ch3
Mara finds the ledger room's spare key hidden inside the clock case.

## ch4
The missing original page is found inside the clock mechanism.
