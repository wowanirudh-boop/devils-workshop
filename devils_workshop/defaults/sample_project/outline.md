# Chapter 1: The Stopped Clock
words: 3200
characters: [Tobias Reed]
Open on the silence: the town wakes and the harbour clock isn't striking.
- Dawn. Mara is fetched to the clock tower; the hands stand at 3:12.
- Tobias insists it's wear and age, and wants it running before the evening tide.
- Mara finds a fresh, bright scratch on the escapement. Not wear: a tool.
- End on Mara pocketing a sliver of brass that shouldn't be there.
